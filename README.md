# Grand Finale Practice

A practice app for the NIDO HUM Spelling Whizz 2026 grand final (ages 6–8, 28 October 2026). It works offline, needs no install, and keeps everything in this browser.

**New in this update:** the official 9–11 word list, a 🔤 **A to Z** tab that covers every word on both lists, a 🔥 **Extremely hard** tab, and a real 📒 **sticker book** (96 stickers, including the yarn 🧶) with stars, a sticker shop and lots more to unlock. Her saved progress, stickers, settings, plan and word changes all carry over.

## On a phone or tablet (from a web address)
- Open the app's web address (for example the GitHub Pages link) in Safari on an iPhone or iPad, or in Chrome on Android. Nothing to install, and no git or GitHub steps on the phone.
- **Put it on the home screen:** in Safari tap Share → **Add to Home Screen**; in Chrome tap ⋮ → **Add to Home screen** (or **Install app**). It then opens full screen like an app, with its own icon.
- **Works offline:** after the first visit, the app keeps a copy on the device, so it opens with no internet.
- **Progress stays on each device.** The phone and the laptop each keep their own progress. To move it, use Parent area → Export and backup: **Download backup** (or **Show the text** and copy it) on one device, then **Restore** on the other (choose the file, or paste the text).
- **Updates:** when the app at the web address is updated, the phone picks it up the next time it is opened with internet (open it twice to be sure). Her progress is kept.
- Printing works best from a laptop.

## Opening the app
- **Laptop (Windows or Mac):** keep the whole folder together and double-click `index.html`. Chrome, Edge or Safari all work.
- **iPad:** copy the folder into the Files app (for example iCloud Drive). Then open `index.html` with Safari. If Files only shows a preview, put the folder on any simple web host and open that link in Safari instead.
- **Android:** copy the folder to the phone and open `index.html` in Chrome. You can also open a hosted copy.
- Tap a button before expecting sound: phones and tablets only allow speech after a tap.
- If the device has no English voice, the app switches to **Parent reads** by itself. A grown-up taps the hidden parent panel to see the word, then reads it aloud.

## A suggested week
- **Every day:** do **Today's practice**. It plays the day's missed words first, then the review words, then new words from the study plan. The daily goal is 40 answers, and you can change it in Settings.
- **From day 5:** play one **Mock Final** a day. Then look at the **Strategy tips** in the Parent area.
- **Spare minutes:** use **A to Z** (one part of 20 words at a time), **Fix my mistakes**, the **Round 1 and Round 2 training** boards, and **Last year's final**.

## The study plan, tiers and strategy tips
- Each round-1 theme (Geography, Animals, Silent letters) has three tiers:
  - **Easy:** the level asked at ages 6–8 in 2025.
  - **Medium:** one step up.
  - **Hard:** includes words asked to ages 9–11.
- **New row: 9–11 list.** Easy ☑ · Medium ☑ · Hard ☑ · Extremely hard ☐. Each box shows mastered / total words.
- The default plan is Easy and Medium of every theme, all round-2 focus words, and the Easy, Medium and Hard 9–11 words: **1,964 words** in total. In the Parent area, tick Hard for the one or two themes she is strongest in.
- **Plan size warning.** Under the plan grid the app says, for example, "At 40 words a day you can cover about 200 of 1,964 words before the final." If the plan is too big, a **Make it fit** button suggests which hard tiers to untick. Nothing changes until you tap it and confirm. The plan is very large compared with the days left, so don't worry about covering it all: Today's practice always puts missed and review words first.
- **Mastered** means the word reached the top review box, with right answers on at least 2 different days.
- **Strategy tips:**
  - **Round 1:** which theme has her best accuracy.
  - **Round 2:** which level earns the most points on average. The formula is right% × points − wrong% × 5. For example, Hard at 70% scores 12.5 a word, which beats Medium at 100% (10 a word).
  - Answers to 9–11 words and A–Z words count too.

## The 9–11 list and its bands
The official 2026 list for ages 9–11 is now in the app. Each 9–11 word has a **band** that says how hard it is for a 6–8-year-old:
- **Easy:** common words she can learn quickly.
- **Medium:** known words with a spelling trap.
- **Hard:** less common or long words; learnable with practice.
- **Extremely hard:** rare, technical or foreign words. They have their own tab and are off the study plan unless you tick them.

Where the 9–11 words show up:
- **Today's practice** takes new words from the 9–11 bands in the plan, in turn with the three themes and round 2.
- **Round 2 training:** after picking a level, choose **Focus (6–8)**, **All 6–8**, or **9–11 list** (the band of that level).
- **Round 1:** some 9–11 words are now animal or silent-letter words, so they appear in those themes.
- **Mock Final round 2** uses focus words plus the plan's 9–11 words of the level she picks. You can change this in Settings → **Mock round 2 uses**: Focus only / Focus + 9–11 (default) / Everything. Extremely hard words only come in if the plan has them ticked.
- **Fix my mistakes** includes 9–11 words.

**Spelling:** the 9–11 list uses British spellings in places (colour, centre, -ise), while the 6–8 list is American (color, center). The app always treats the spelling in the word list as correct. When a word has a spelling difference, a note is shown after Check, for example "British spelling. American spelling: enroll."

## 🔤 The A to Z tab
This is for practising **every word on both lists** (2,990 words), letter by letter.
- **The A–Z screen** shows a tile for each letter with "done / total", a progress ring and a ✓ when the letter is complete. At the top: "Letters done: 7 of 26 · Words done: 812 of 2,990".
- **Choices** (remembered; also in Parent area → Settings):
  - **List:** 6–8, 9–11, or Both (default).
  - **Extremely hard words:** on (default) or off. When off, each tile says how many are hidden, for example "+12 extremely hard (hidden)", so nothing is silently missing.
- **Tap a letter** to see its words in ABC order, split into **parts of 20**, for example "Part 3 of 9: bewilder – blizzard". Each part shows done, in progress or not started. **Start / Continue** plays the next unfinished part, and **Continue** on the A–Z screen goes back to where she was.
- **Word list** shows every word in the letter with ✓ done, ✗ needs practice, or • not yet, with a 🔊 button and the meaning.
- **Playing a part** uses the normal question card. Words come in ABC order (Settings has "Shuffle within each part", off by default). A word she gets wrong goes to the end of the part and comes back once. If it's wrong again, it goes on the "needs practice" list and the part moves on, so a part always finishes. It resumes exactly where she stopped, even after a reload.

**What "done" means**
- A word is **done** once she spells it right in the A–Z tab (or on the letter's "missed words" board). Once done, it stays done.
- A part is **finished** when every word in it has been asked.
- A letter is **complete** when all its words (with the current choices) are done. "Needs practice" words keep a letter from completing until she gets them right, in a later pass or with **Practise this letter's missed words**.

**Rewards:** each finished part opens a sticker pack, each complete letter wins its **golden letter** for the sticker book, and finishing every letter wins the **A to Z Champion** trophy. A–Z answers also count towards the normal review progress.

To start A–Z again, use Parent area → Reset → **Reset A–Z progress** (nothing else is touched).

## 🔥 The Extremely hard tab
- The 214 extremely hard 9–11 words, as a short board game. Pick a letter range: A–F, G–M, N–S or T–Z.
- **See every word** lists them all with 🔊 and the meaning, so you can decide whether she should learn them.
- They are off the study plan by default. Tick **9–11 list · Extremely hard** in the plan if you want them in Today's practice (and in the mock).

## 📒 Stickers, stars and the shop
- **Stars:** every right answer earns ⭐ 1. Her stars show at the top of the screen.
- **3 right in a row:** 1 sticker. A 🔥 meter shows her current run, with cheers at 3, 5 and 10.
- **Finishing a board, an A–Z part, or Today's goal:** a **pack** of 3 stickers. The cards flip one by one with a little sound, and new ones get a "NEW!" ribbon.
- **Every 20 answers in one go:** a 🎁 surprise box she taps to open (1 or 2 stickers).
- **Sticker shop:** she can spend **15 stars** to choose any sticker she doesn't have yet. That is how she can always get the yarn.
- **Rarity:** most stickers are common; rare ones have a sparkly border and legendary ones are gold and shining. New stickers she doesn't have yet come first, and she never gets the same sticker twice in a row.
- **The sticker book** has a page for each of the 8 packs (Cosy crafts, Sweet treats, Animal friends, Ocean, Garden, Space, Magic, Fun and games), with collected stickers in colour and missing ones as "?" slots. Tap a sticker to see it big. A **Badges** page has the 26 golden letters and the trophies: first Mock Final, 130, 140 and 150 points, a new personal best, a 3-day streak, and A to Z Champion.
- **Unlocks:** each completed pack unlocks a new **board theme** (for example Candy land, Ocean, Space, Garden), and every 10 different stickers unlock a new **board token**. Choose them on the **My board** page.
- **Ollie the owl** cheers her on with lots of different lines (never the same twice in a row) and says a short cheer every few right answers. You can turn the spoken cheer off in Settings.
- **Brain break:** after 20 minutes of practice the app offers a one-minute stretch ("Reach up high! Touch your toes!"). A grown-up can tap **Parent: skip**, or turn it off in Settings.
- **My progress** now has a practice calendar with a sticker on every day she practised, up to the final.
- Sound effects stay gentle and can be muted with the 🔔 button. Animations calm down if the device is set to reduce motion.

## Adding words and printing lists
- To get in, press and hold the **Parent area** button for 3 seconds.
- **Word editor:** search, fix, hide or add words. Your changes are kept separately from the original list, so they are never lost when the list is updated. Filters include the 9–11 list, the 9–11 band, and **Needs review** (9 words whose text was only partly readable in the scan).
- **Import words:** load a CSV file (`data/add-words-template.csv` shows the format, and `words.csv` works too), paste a list of words, or load a JSON file. You will see a preview before anything is saved.
  - **Missing 9–11 words:** the scan of the 9–11 list has no A or N pages and no first G page. When you have those words, import them with **New words are on: The 9–11 list**. Pick a band, or let the app guess one from the word (it never guesses "Extremely hard"). They go straight into the A–Z tab, and the A–Z counts update. A CSV can also have `sources` and `band_9_11` columns.
  - A word you added earlier without a list can be put on a list by importing it again with the list chosen.
- **Print lists:** choose a theme and tier, a silent-letter pattern, a round-2 level, a 9–11 band, an A–Z letter, or the missed words, then tap Print.

## Backup and restore
- Your progress lives only in this browser. Every 100 answers or 3 days, the Home screen reminds you to **Download backup**.
- Backups now include the A–Z progress and the sticker book. Backups made before this update still restore.
- To restore, go to Parent area → Export and backup → Restore. You will see what is in the backup and be asked to confirm. The current data is saved in the browser first.

## Updating the word list (for grown-ups comfortable with a computer)
1. Edit or replace `data/words.json` (schema_version 2).
2. Run `node tools/build-words-js.mjs`. It checks the list and rebuilds `js/data/words.js`, then prints the counts (all words, each list, A–Z words per letter, 9–11 bands, theme tiers). If anything is wrong, it stops and names the record.
3. Run `node tools/build-sw.mjs` so phones pick up the change (it updates the offline copy list in `sw.js`).
4. Run the tests with `node --test` (no install needed). For the optional browser checks, run `node tools/browser-check.mjs` (laptop) and `node tools/mobile-check.mjs` (every screen on 7 phone and tablet sizes, plus offline). Both need Node 18 or newer, and Chrome or Edge.

## Decisions
- **Mastery days:** each word's progress also stores which days it was answered right (`days`), which is how "right on 2 different days" is counted.
- **Today's practice:**
  - It uses the board length setting. Reaching the trophy starts a new lap, and the session continues until the daily goal is met.
  - Try-again, due and new words all come from the study plan only.
  - New words rotate through five streams: Geography, Animals, Silent letters, Round 2, and the 9–11 list.
- **Speech:**
  - **Meaning** is spoken with the word replaced by "blank". **Sentence** is spoken in full, because that is what the pronouncer does. On screen, both are always shown masked.
  - The 8-second speech watchdog gets extra time for long sentences, so they are not cut off.
- **Hidden word on screen:** while a question is showing, the current word is masked anywhere else on the page as well, including button labels, notices and the page title. For example, if the word is "answer", the "Undo last answer" button is masked. Masking too much is safer than too little. Sticker packs and brain breaks only pop up when no question is showing.
- **Timer:** after a page reload, the timer waits until the word is said again.
- **Mock Final:**
  - If the plan has no tier ticked for a theme, the mock uses all tiers of that theme.
  - If a round-2 level is not in the plan, the mock still uses that level's focus words.
  - "Mock round 2 uses: Focus only" means the plan's round-2 words (focus words unless "focus words only" is unticked in the plan). "Focus + 9–11" adds the plan's 9–11 words of the chosen level. "Everything" uses every 6–8 and 9–11 word of the level. Extremely hard words come in only if the plan ticks them, whatever the setting.
  - Confetti appears when the total reaches at least 130 (a 2025 podium score). The 130, 140 and 150 trophies use "at least".
  - Once a mock's results are saved, its answers can no longer be undone.
  - "Voice unclear, new word" needs a 1.2-second press, so a child can't use it to skip words. In the A–Z tab it moves the word to the end of the part, with no penalty.
- **Added words:**
  - Words added by the parent get the source `added` and the text source `added_by_parent`. Words added as 9–11 or 6–8 words also get that list's source, so they are A–Z words.
  - Edits to existing words keep their original sources. Which list a word from the official list is on (and its 9–11 band) can't be changed by an edit.
  - When an import matches a word, a default theme is added to the word's existing themes rather than replacing them.
  - A 9–11 word imported without a difficulty gets one that matches its band (Easy 1, Medium 2, Hard 4, Extremely hard 5), so its round-2 level matches the band.
- **Word bank export:** hidden words are left out of the exported word bank, except words from the 2025 final. The CSV has the same columns as the new `words.csv`.
- **Phone board:** on screens narrower than 900 px, the board is a compact strip above the question with smaller square numbers. The buttons and text still meet the 56 px and 20 px minimums.
- **The 9–11 list in pools:** words only on the 9–11 list come into round 2 through the 9–11 row of the plan, not through "focus words only" being unticked, so the row really adds and removes them. Words on both lists keep their 6–8 level and count as 6–8 words. Extremely hard words are never in a round-1 theme pool.
- **Updating from the previous version:** her plan keeps every tick and gains the 9–11 row with its default ticks (Easy, Medium, Hard). The stickers she had already earned are moved into the sticker book as the same stickers she saw before (the old 🎵 became the 🎤 microphone), and the sticker count stays the same.
- **Old board stickers:** every 5th square no longer gives a sticker (stickers now come from runs, packs, boxes and the shop). Those squares still show a milestone picture from the board theme.
- **A–Z:**
  - Words the parent has hidden are left out of the A–Z tab, like everywhere else.
  - Golden letters and the A to Z Champion trophy are awarded for the list choice in use at the time, and are kept even if the choice changes later.
  - A letter whose words are all hidden by the extremely-hard switch still shows its tile (with the hidden count) but isn't counted in "Letters done".
  - "Practise this letter's missed words" is a normal board; a right answer there marks the word done in A–Z.
  - "Reset A–Z progress" keeps the golden letters already won.
- **Stickers:**
  - Each pack has 8 common, 3 rare and 1 legendary sticker. A draw first picks the rarity (70% / 25% / 5%), then a sticker of that rarity, preferring ones she doesn't have.
  - The stickers in a pack or surprise box go into the book straight away; opening it only shows them. Undoing the answer that earned them takes them back.
  - "3 in a row" gives a sticker at every 3rd right answer of a run (3, 6, 9…). "Every 20 answers in one go" counts answers in the current board, part or mock.
  - The board token is her avatar until she picks an unlocked token.
  - Some newer emoji (the needle, coral, seal, pot plant, magic wand, bubbles and roller skate) can't be drawn by older devices such as Windows 10. There the app shows a similar emoji instead (for example a safety pin for the needle); the sticker's name doesn't change.
- **Brain break:** practice time counts the time between answers (gaps of 3 minutes or more don't count). Skipping counts as a break, so it isn't offered again for another 20 minutes.
- **Reset → Clear practice progress** now also clears the A–Z progress and the sticker book (stars, stickers, badges, trophies).
- **Phones:**
  - The 3-second parent hold listens to the finger directly, because a long press otherwise opens the phone's own menu and cancels the hold.
  - On narrow screens the study plan shows each tick above its count, so all four columns fit without swiping.
  - The home-screen details and the offline copy are only used when the app is opened from a web address; opening `index.html` from the folder works as before.
  - When the app is shown inside another page (some hosting sites do this), printing is hidden and backups are shown as text to copy, because such pages block printing and downloads. Restore accepts pasted backup text as well as a file.

## Manual checklist (if `tools/browser-check.mjs` can't run)
Open `index.html` from the folder, then:
1. Answer 10 words of Today's practice.
2. Play a full Mock Final using ✓, ✗ and Undo.
3. Make sure the word never shows before you tap Check.
4. Reload the page in the middle of a mock and check that it resumes at the same word.
5. Import `data/add-words-template.csv`.
6. Download a backup.
7. Print one tier.
8. Open A to Z and check the header says 2,990 words. Turn extremely hard words off and check the tiles show "(hidden)".
9. Play one part of the letter with the fewest words (X), getting one word wrong. Check the wrong word comes back at the end.
10. Reload in the middle of the part and check it resumes at the same word.
11. Finish the part: a sticker pack opens, and X becomes a golden letter.
12. In the sticker book, open the shop and buy the yarn (you need 15 stars). Check the "collected" count goes up.
13. Open the Extremely hard tab and start the A–F board.
